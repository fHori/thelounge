# Credits

Horizon Lounge builds on years of open-source work by the upstream IRC community.

## Upstream projects

- **[The Lounge](https://github.com/thelounge/thelounge)** ([thelounge.chat](https://thelounge.chat)) — The original self-hosted web IRC client, created and maintained by The Lounge authors and contributors (`xPaw`, `astorije`, `erming`, `brunnre8`, `MaxLeiter`, `AlMcKinlay`, and the broader contributor community).
- **[Tetrahydroc](https://github.com/tetrahydroc)** ([tetrahydroc/thelounge](https://github.com/tetrahydroc/thelounge)) — For his work on his fork of The Lounge, which Horizon Lounge directly builds upon, including multi-upload backend groundwork, tracker features, and early quality-of-life improvements.
